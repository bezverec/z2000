# z2000 0.2.0-rc.2

This release candidate widens Part 1 decode and encode coverage, opens most
of the TIFF input that scanners and archives produce, and moves the build to
Zig 0.17, while keeping unsupported input fail-closed.

## Highlights Since 0.2.0-rc.1

- Broader Part 1 decode: tile-local COD/COC/QCD/QCC overrides, mixed and
  component-local transforms, RGN Maxshift ROI, CRG, PLM, CAP/PRF, general
  multipart POC across inline, PPT, and PPM headers, every TLM width, empty
  resolutions and subbands, arithmetic bypass across layers, irreversible
  9/7 and RGBA through the native and planar paths, and a native decode
  path for any bounded component count with signed or unsigned reversible
  components up to 29 bits, written as raw PGX or ZRAW diagnostics. A
  manifest-driven Part 1 corpus pins
  Kakadu, OpenJPEG, and Grok streams, including fail-closed producer
  defects.
- Selective and bounded decode: tile, reference-region, quality-layer, and
  reduced-resolution selection, with streaming JP2-to-TIFF output for
  palette, alpha, subsampled, and grayscale layouts.
- Broader encode: gray, gray+alpha, and RGBA on tile grids, RGB without MCT,
  BYPASS without TERMALL, odd and tiny tiles at the global level count, TLM
  over many segments, planar 9/7, and every unsigned precision from 1 to
  16 bits. A writer defect that put EPH after the packet body in
  one-part-per-tile layouts is fixed, and the decoder now checks EPH where
  ISO A.8.2 puts it.
- TIFF input: 1- to 16-bit samples, LZW, Deflate, PackBits, and baseline
  JPEG (gray, RGB, YCbCr 1x1/2x1/2x2) compression with horizontal
  prediction, tiled and separate-plane layouts, and multi-page files, one
  JP2 per page (`out-p001.jp2`, ...) or `--page N`. Batch conversion refuses
  output-name collisions, including page names and names differing only in
  case, before writing anything.
- PNG input keeps 1/2/4-bit grayscale at its native depth and accepts the
  sRGB-valued `cHRM`/`gAMA` chunks ImageMagick writes.
- Hardening: a truncated zlib stream could overrun the input in Zig 0.16's
  inflater (a panic in safe builds, an out-of-bounds read in ReleaseFast)
  through PNG or TIFF Deflate input; z2000 guards against it, and Zig 0.17
  fixes it upstream. Fuzz campaigns over the committed fixtures end in
  errors, never panics or hangs.
- Builds with Zig 0.17.0.

## Release Candidate Policy

The CLI and Zig API remain unstable before 1.0. This is not a general JP2/JPX,
camera RAW, or HDR converter. Each input adapter has a deliberately narrow
documented envelope; old-style or progressive JPEG, CCITT, LZMA, and ZSTD
TIFF compression, Adam7 PNG, CFA/demosaicing, compressed or tiled EXR,
general ICC transforms, extended XMP, and semantic metadata rewriting remain
unsupported. JPEG-compressed TIFF is decoded from a lossy source before
encoding. JP2 metadata payloads are preserved but are not yet restored into
TIFF output.

The project's 100/100 ISO engineering scorecards are internal coverage tools,
not formal ISO conformance certification. The binaries are unsigned; verify
the platform archive against `SHA256SUMS`. Every archive contains identical
`z2000` and `z2k` executables for the long and short command names.
